Legbook: Pilot Logbook

A flight logbook for pilots on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Log Out, Off, On and In in UTC or local time, let the app work out night time, add your own columns, and track the currency rules that apply to you. Your logbook stays on your device: no account, no server, no data collected.

An unofficial app, not affiliated with or endorsed by any civil aviation authority.
