Legbook: Pilot Logbook

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how Kiyoaki Segawa (“the developer”, “we”) handles information in the app Legbook: Pilot Logbook for iPhone and iPad, including its Apple Watch app, widgets and watch-face complications (“the app”).

1. Summary

The developer does not collect any personal data through the app. The app has no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your logbook is stored on your devices and, only if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud.

2. Information we collect

None. In particular:

  • There is no account registration or sign-in.
  • The developer runs no server for the app. The app does not send your records to the developer or to any third party.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software (SDK) and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Flights, settings and other records you enter are stored on your device in the app’s own database (SQLite).
  • Deleting the app deletes the data stored on that device.
  • Files you export (backups, CSV files) go where you choose (for example the Files app or e-mail). How they are handled there is governed by that destination’s terms.
  • When you import, the app reads only the files you pick.
  • If you use a device backup (such as iCloud Backup), the app’s data may be included in that backup.
  • Apple Watch: if you install the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends data (recent flights, currency status, totals and map data such as airports and routes) directly to your paired Apple Watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework, device to device. It does not pass through the developer, and the developer receives nothing. The data is stored on the watch; nothing is sent back from the watch. Deleting the app from the watch deletes that data.

4. iCloud sync (optional)

  • Only on devices where you turn on iCloud sync in the app, a copy of your records is stored in the app’s container in your own iCloud (an Apple service) and synced between your iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • The developer cannot see or retrieve data in your iCloud. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud.
  • You can turn sync off at any time. Data left in iCloud can be deleted from your device’s Settings (iCloud storage management).

5. Purchases and subscriptions

  • Purchases and subscriptions (Pro, including any free trial) are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.
  • The app checks whether you have Pro on your device, using the App Store’s purchase records (StoreKit). The result is not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • You manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

6. Notifications, widgets and watch faces

  • Reminders (for example of expiry dates) are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notifications are used. Permission is requested only when you turn reminders on.
  • What the widgets show (including map images) is prepared by the app on your device and kept in a storage area on the device shared only by the app and its widgets.
  • What the watch-face complications show is kept on your Apple Watch, in an area shared only by the watch app and its complications.

7. Maps and time zones

Maps, airport positions, time zones and night-time calculations use data built into the app. They make no network request.

8. Information provided by Apple

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in your device’s settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymized usage and crash information. This is done by Apple, not sent by the app, and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

9. Sharing with third parties

As we do not collect personal data, we have none to share, sell or transfer. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

10. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Because the developer does not collect or process your personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object to. Your data stays under your control:

  • view, correct and delete records in the app (Settings also has an option to delete all data);
  • export your data at any time (CSV, backup file);
  • delete iCloud data as described in section 4, and the app’s data by deleting the app.

If you contact us by e-mail, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Children

The app is a logbook for pilots and is not directed to children. Since it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

12. Security

Your data is protected by your device’s security features (passcode, data protection) and, if you use iCloud sync, by Apple’s iCloud security. Keep your exported files and backups in a safe place.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change would mean that the app starts collecting data, we will say so in the app before it happens.
