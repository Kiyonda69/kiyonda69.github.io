Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Kiyoaki Segawa (“the developer”, “we”) handles information in the app Jiriri for iPhone and iPad, including its Apple Watch app and widgets (“the app”).

1. Summary

  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not receive any of your data.
  • Your wake-up sets, wake-up records and mission targets are stored on your device and in your own iCloud, if iCloud is on for the app.
  • Sleep data from Apple Health and events from Calendar are read only on your device, only if you turn those features on, and are never sent anywhere.
  • No leaderboards, Game Center or friend features. Your score and streaks are shown only to you.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Wake-up sets (time, repeat days, ringing settings, missions, sounds), wake-up records (when the alarm started, when you dismissed it, which ring, missions, time taken and score) and the alarms currently set.
  • Mission targets you register: barcode or QR code values with a name, and reference photos.
  • Sounds you import or record, stored in the app’s own folder.
  • Photos taken during the photo mission are compared with your reference photo on the device and are not saved.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. Device backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include the app’s data.
  • Apple Watch and widgets: your iPhone sends the data they show (your wake-up sets, next alarm, level and streak) directly to your paired Apple Watch, and keeps widget data in a storage area on the device shared only by the app and its widgets.

3. iCloud sync

  • If iCloud is on for the app, your wake-up sets, wake-up records and mission targets (including reference photos) are stored in the app’s private container in your own iCloud and synced between your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Imported or recorded sounds and the alarms currently set are not synced.
  • The developer cannot see or retrieve data in your private iCloud. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for the app and delete its data in your device’s iCloud settings.

4. Permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): to ring your wake-up alarms.
  • Camera: only for the barcode and photo missions and for registering their targets.
  • Microphone: only when you record your own alarm sound.
  • Apple Health (optional): with your permission, the app reads your sleep analysis to show it next to your wake-up records. It does not write to Health. Health data stays on your device, is not stored in iCloud by the app, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • Calendar (optional): with your permission, the app looks at the first timed event of the next day to suggest a wake-up time. Event details are not stored or sent.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings.

5. Information provided by Apple

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in your device’s settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymized usage and crash information. This is done by Apple, not sent by the app, and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, and the app sends no data to the developer or to any third party. Information leaves your device only through Apple’s services that you choose to use (iCloud, Apple Health, Apple Watch).

7. Your rights

Your data stays under your control: view and delete records and targets in the app, delete iCloud data as described in section 3, and delete the app’s data by deleting the app. If you contact us by e-mail, we use your message and address only to answer you. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
