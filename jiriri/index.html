Jiriri: Wake-Up Alarm

A wake-up alarm for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that keeps ringing until you are really up. Tap one of your wake-up sets and Jiriri rings every minute (or at the interval you choose) through your wake-up window — for example 5:00 to 5:10 — and keeps ringing afterwards. Stopping a ring only silences that ring; to stop them all, finish a short game: math, memory panels, a slide puzzle, reflex taps, shaking, scanning a barcode or photographing a place you registered.

Earn points for getting up early, level up, keep your streak and collect badges. Free, with no ads and no account.
