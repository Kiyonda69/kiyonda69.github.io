Frequently asked questions

How do I set an alarm?

Tap a wake-up set on the Alarm tab. It is armed for its next time (and, if it repeats, again after you dismiss it). Long-press a set to edit or delete it, or tap New set. You can also arm a set from a widget, Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button or Siri (“Set wake-up alarm”).

Why does it keep ringing after I tap Stop?

That is the point of Jiriri. Each ring in your window (for example every minute from 5:00 to 5:10) is a separate alarm, and it keeps ringing for the extra time you set until you dismiss it. Stop only silences the current ring. To stop them all, tap Dismiss on the alarm (or open Jiriri) and finish the missions. Before the first ring you can cancel freely.

The alarm did not ring.

  • Check that alarms are allowed: Settings › Apps › Jiriri › Alarms. Jiriri shows a warning when they are off.
  • Alarms ring even in Silent mode and Focus. Check the alarm volume in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
  • Deleting the app removes its alarms.

How do the barcode and photo missions work?

Register a target first in Settings › Mission targets — for example the barcode on your toothpaste, or a photo of your sink taken from where you will stand in the morning. In the morning you have to get up and scan the same code or take a matching photo. Photos are compared on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Can I use my own sound?

Yes. In Settings › Alarm sounds, import an audio file from Files or record your own (up to 30 seconds). Songs from Apple Music cannot be used as alarm sounds. A set can switch to a stronger sound after a number of rings.

How are points and levels calculated?

You score more the earlier the ring you get up on, the faster you finish the missions and the harder the difficulty, with a bonus for your streak. Getting up after the window still scores, but less, and breaks your streak.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Your alarms also alert on your paired Apple Watch. The Watch app shows your next alarm, lets you arm a wake-up set, and lets you dismiss with math, reflex taps or shaking.

Is it free?

Yes. Jiriri has no in-app purchases, ads or account.
